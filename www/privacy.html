Privacy policy

PauzFirst · Last updated November 13, 2026

Data we collect
Account data
Your name, email address and password (stored securely by Supabase), or your Google account name and email if you sign in with Google.
App data
Your question lists, presets, session history and stats.
AI-generated questions
If you use the AI feature, the prompt you type is sent to Google Gemini through our server to generate questions.
Permissions
Camera and microphone
The camera is used only for Mirror mode, which shows your own face while you train. Recording is optional: a session recording is saved to your Movies folder (Movies/Pauzfirst) and audio-only recordings to your Music folder (Music/Pauzfirst). Recordings stay on your device and are not uploaded.
Notifications and alarms
Used for the reminders you set.
Internet
Used to sign in and sync your data.
How we use your data

Only to run the app: sign you in, save and sync your data, and send your reminders. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Third-party services
Supabase
Authentication and database hosting.
Google Sign-In
Optional sign-in.
Google Gemini
Generates questions when you use the AI feature.
Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all its data from the Profile screen, or request deletion by email and we will delete it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
