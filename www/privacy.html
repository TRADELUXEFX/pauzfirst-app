PauzFirst Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

PauzFirst ("the app") is an Android app. It helps you pause and think before you respond. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and password (stored securely by our backend provider, Supabase), or your Google account name and email if you sign in with Google.
  • App data: your question lists, presets, session history and stats.
  • AI-generated questions: if you use the AI feature, the prompt you type is sent to Google Gemini through our server to generate questions.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: used only if you choose to record a session. Recordings are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Notifications and alarms: used for your reminders.
  • Internet: used to sign in and sync your data.

How we use your data

Only to run the app: sign you in, save and sync your data across sessions, and send the reminders you set. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Third-party services

  • Supabase: authentication and database hosting.
  • Google Sign-In: optional sign-in.
  • Google Gemini: generates questions when you use the AI feature.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and all associated data, delete it from the Profile screen in the app or email us at the address below and we will delete it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
