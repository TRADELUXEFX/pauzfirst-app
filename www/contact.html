Delete your account and data
In the app
Open Profile and choose Delete account. This removes your account and all its data.
By email
Send a request from the email address linked to your account (or tell us the email you signed up with, including if you used Google sign-in). We will delete your account and all associated data, including your question lists, presets, session history and stats.
Request account deletion
What gets deleted
Your account details (name, email, sign-in) and all your data stored on our servers. Session recordings are kept only on your device and are never uploaded, so uninstalling the app removes them.
